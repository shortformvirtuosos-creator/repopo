# Build a Profitable AI Business From Bosnia

A beginner in Bosnia and Herzegovina makes the most money with AI by **selling finished results to businesses**: short AI video ads and content packs, AI receptionists and WhatsApp bots, "get recommended by ChatGPT" visibility fixes, and AI-built websites. Waiting for faceless channels or courses to pay off is slower and less certain. The tools are cheap. A starter stack of an AI assistant, Google AI Pro, ElevenLabs, Suno and Higgsfield Plus costs **about €91 a month**, and **one 300–600 KM local video job pays for it**. Local budgets are small (the average net wage was **1,706 KM in June 2026**), so use Bosnian clients for speed and proof. Then sell the same services to the diaspora of roughly two million people and to EU and US clients at two to five times the price. **Higgsfield is your production studio.** The business owner never touches it; they pay you for the finished video file. Low-ticket digital products only work once you control serious attention: **about 1 million views a month yields roughly $2,000 net** in a realistic funnel. To get paid, use bank transfer in EUR, Gumroad, Polar or Lemon Squeezy. Stripe merchant accounts are not available in BiH, and PayPal pays out only to Visa cards. After six focused months, a realistic income is **about 5,000 KM a month before tax**. The conservative case is near the average wage and the aggressive case is above 12,000 KM.

## Service businesses beat passive AI income for a beginner

Business owners don't want "AI". They want more customers, fewer missed calls and ads that sell. Every model below that produces fast, reliable income sells a finished outcome that AI makes cheap to produce. Higgsfield's own numbers show where the money sits. Its CEO says **about 70% of revenue comes from agencies** ([SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/500m-arr-60-engineers-cash-flow-positive-how-higgsfield-actually-runs-with-ceo-alex-mashrabov/)). One agency reports spending **$3,000–$50,000 a month on credits** to produce variations of content for clients' social media ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/lisa-bonos/how-higgsfield-became-one-of-the-fastest-growing-ai-video-platforms-for-creators-and-startups/91397929)). Businesses paying someone to make AI content for them is the market.

Tools cost little next to client prices. A polished 30-second AI ad uses roughly **$40–$250 of credits** (worked estimate in the Higgsfield section), and direct clients pay **$300–$800** for one ([Playcut](https://playcut.ai/blog/how-much-to-charge-for-ai-video/)). So the "tool ROI" looks enormous for every service. The real limits are your hours, your taste and your ability to sell. The table therefore also shows estimated earnings per hour.

**Ranking for a Bosnian beginner.**

1. **AI video ads and content packs.** The demo sells itself in a WhatsApp message, margins are high, and Higgsfield's agency-heavy revenue shows that buyers exist.
2. **AI receptionists and messaging bots.** These carry the highest recurring value per client and you can prove the ROI. They need more technical tinkering, and voice quality in Bosnian, Croatian and Serbian is unproven.
3. **AI visibility work.** It is the cheapest and fastest way to get in the door, but local tickets are small.
4. **Websites.** A familiar product that is easy to add on.
5. **Foreign UGC ad packs.** The highest ceiling, but the hardest first sale.

Social media management is commoditized. Faceless brands and products are things to build **in parallel**, not your first income. Choose **one niche and one core offer for 90 days**. Spreading across many offers is the failure pattern practitioners describe most often: buying a template, selling breadth, and having no proof ([ciela.ai summary of Reddit discussions](https://ciela.ai/blogs/ai-automation-agency-reddit-honest-truth)).

## Higgsfield is your studio; the business pays for the finished video

### What Higgsfield is, in plain words

Higgsfield is a website and app with a monthly subscription. In one workspace it gives you many of the best AI video and image models: Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, Seedance, Wan, plus its own Soul and DoP models ([Higgsfield AI Video](https://higgsfield.ai/ai-video)). It adds ad-specific tools on top. **UGC Factory** turns a product into a creator-style ad using templates such as Unboxing, Product Review, Try-On and Tutorial ([Higgsfield blog](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/faceless-videos-ugc-ads-2026)). **Lipsync Studio** makes a face speak a script. **Higgsfield Ads** turns a product URL into a video in about two minutes ([Tadka review](https://tadkai.io/resources/higgsfield-ads-review-2026)). **Soul ID** keeps one character's face consistent across clips. It is a big, real company: it reported a **$5.4B valuation and $700M annualized revenue in August 2026** (a company claim, not audited) ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/higgsfield-series-b-400m-5-4bn-valuation-700m-revenue)).

### How a business owner actually pays for Higgsfield output

The owner of a Sarajevo café never logs into Higgsfield and doesn't need to know it exists. **They pay you for finished video files** (MP4s in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9) that they post on Instagram and Facebook or run as ads. The money flows like this:

1. The café pays you, say, **490 KM a month** by bank transfer for six Reels.
2. You pay Higgsfield **$49 a month** plus about $20 for the rest of your tools.
3. You keep the difference.

Higgsfield says paid plans don't restrict commercial use and that rights to exported outputs survive cancellation. That comes from a marketing post, so read the Terms ([Higgsfield trust post](https://geo.higgsfield.ai/blog/recommended/can-i-trust-higgsfield-with-a-paid-subscription-6f890c)).

Why wouldn't the owner just do it themselves? Because good AI video takes many attempts and real taste. The famous **$2,000 Kalshi ad that aired during the NBA Finals took 300–400 generations to get 15 usable clips**, across 2–3 days of work ([CO/AI](https://getcoai.com/news/kalshis-nba-finals-ad-costs-just-2k-using-googles-ai-video-tool/)). The client paid for **concept, taste and speed, not computing power**. That is what you sell too.

### Genjutsu: recasting real footage instead of inventing it

**Genjutsu is real.** It is Higgsfield's video-to-video tool, launched around **1 September 2026**, with two modes ([Higgsfield blog](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/higgsfield-genjutsu); [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/en/higgsfield-genjutsu)):

- **Motion Transfer** keeps the original motion, camera and timing, and rebuilds the characters, locations and style from your reference images.
- **Object Swap** replaces one item, such as an outfit, a product, a character or a location, and leaves the rest of the shot untouched.

It takes reference clips of **3–30 seconds** and up to **40 reference images**, and outputs up to 1080p. It is not a text-to-video tool and not a face-swap tool. A 15-second clip costs about **40 credits (~$2) at 480p, 104 (~$5.20) at 720p and 144 (~$7.20) at 1080p**, and re-rolls are charged too ([Kavel.ai](https://www.kavel.ai/blog/higgsfield-genjutsu-price-guide)). Reviewers report renders stuck on "processing" for days, and hands and heads that blur in fast motion ([AIChief](https://aichief.com/ai-video-tools/higgsfield-genjutsu/)).

For business owners, Genjutsu turns footage they already have into more assets:

- A boutique films one model walking and gets five outfit versions with Object Swap.
- A furniture maker swaps fabric or wood finishes in a single showroom clip.
- A restaurant restyles a phone clip into a seasonal or cinematic look.
- A creator who won't show their face films themselves and comes out as a branded character.

Don't use it to misrepresent a real property or product. A restyled apartment that doesn't match reality is a complaint waiting to happen.

### Credits run out fast, so price per finished asset

Higgsfield credits are worth about **$0.043–$0.05 each**. Premium models reportedly cost **40–70 credits per generation**, which is **$2–$3.50 per attempt** ([Krea](https://www.krea.ai/blog/higgsfield-pricing-explained-2026-unlimited-credits-and-real-monthly-costs)). So Plus's 1,000 credits buy only about **14–25 premium generations**, or about **six 1080p Genjutsu clips**. Subscription credits **do not roll over** ([Camclo3d](https://camclo3d.com/blog/higgsfield-pricing)). Plan names and prices changed at least three times in 2026, so quote clients per finished asset and never by plan name.

Budget it like this:

- Draft each shot 4–6 times on cheap or free models: Google Flow's 50 free daily credits, Veo Lite or Fast, Kling, or Seedance Fast.
- Render 1–2 finals on premium models.
- That works out to roughly **55–110 Higgsfield credits ($2.50–$5.50) per finished clip**.

On that basis a simple 15-second local ad costs about **$15–$60** in credits. A polished 30-second ad costs **$40–$250**, depending on the model mix. These are this report's estimates, built from the unit prices above.

## AI receptionists and automations sell revenue a business already loses

### Three offers with a number you can prove

The automation offers with the clearest ROI recover money a business is already losing.

**Missed-call capture.** The widely repeated claim that **62% of small-business calls go unanswered** comes from a 2016 study ([getaira](https://www.getaira.io/blog/missed-business-calls-statistics)). Newer platform data for home services puts the figure at **27%**, and says **fewer than 3%** of callers sent to voicemail leave a message ([Magicline](https://www.magicline.ai/blog/ai-receptionist-statistics-2026)). Use those figures only as openers, and build the pitch on the prospect's own numbers.

**Speed to lead.** The MIT/InsideSales study of about 15,000 leads found that calling within **5 minutes instead of 30** made a lead **100x more likely to be reached and 21x more likely to qualify** ([Callsetter](https://callsetter.ai/blog/speed-to-lead)). An instant WhatsApp or SMS reply to every Facebook lead form is an easy product to sell.

**Database reactivation.** AI messages a client's old list and books interested people, often priced **per booked appointment**, so the client takes no risk. One provider reports **4.4% of dormant leads booking on average (peak 8.9%)**, and warns that promises of 15–20% are a red flag ([LeadsNow](https://leadsnow.ai/how-to-run-a-database-reactivation-campaign/)). You need consent to send marketing messages.

The pitch is a short calculation done with the owner, using their own numbers. Here is an illustration for a clinic:

- 20 missed calls a week × 4.33 weeks × 25% who would have booked × 100 KM per first visit ≈ **2,165 KM lost a month**.
- If an AI receptionist recovers half of that, the clinic gains ~1,080 KM for a 350 KM fee, about **3x its money back**.

A reactivation example: 1,500 old patients × 4.4% = **66 booked visits**. At 20 KM per booking you earn 1,320 KM, and the clinic gets 66 appointments. Every input here is an assumption, so swap in the client's real figures.

Language-model costs barely register. Anthropic lists its cheapest current model at **$0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens**, and its mid-tier at $2/$10 ([Anthropic pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing.md)). By this report's arithmetic, 1,000 chats of about 3,000 input and 500 output tokens cost **under $1 on the cheapest tier, or about $11 on the mid-tier**. On WhatsApp, Meta bills per template message by country. Replies inside the customer-initiated 24-hour window were free, and a reported 1 October 2026 change charges service replies only after **1,000 free per number per month** ([EngageLab](https://www.engagelab.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing)). n8n Cloud Starter allows 2,500 executions. A workflow that checks for new data every 10 minutes uses **4,320 executions a month** and breaks the cap, so self-host n8n or use triggers instead ([Jetadmin](https://www.jetadmin.io/blog/n8n-pricing/)).

### What to charge: three markets

US guides put small-business automation at **$1,500–$5,000 setup plus $500–$1,500 a month** ([Layer3Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/roi/ai-automation-agency-cost)). Newcomers are told to start at **$800–$3,500 setup plus $400–$900 a month** ([Duet](https://duet.so/blog/turn-ai-agent-builds-into-recurring-retainers)). Managed voice agents list at **$2,000–$10,000 setup plus $500–$2,500 a month**. Off-the-shelf AI receptionist software costs **$49–$299 a month** (Rosie, Goodcall, Smith.ai from $150 for 75 calls), and that sets a ceiling on prices for simple setups ([Cloudtalk](https://www.cloudtalk.io/smith-ai-pricing/); [Dialzara](https://dialzara.com/compare/rosie-vs-smith-ai)).

Your premium must come from integration, local-language tuning and the fact that you manage it. Suggested prices:

| Offer | BiH local | Diaspora / EU |
|---|---|---|
| FAQ chatbot (website, Instagram, WhatsApp) | 500–1,000 KM setup + 100–250 KM/mo | €600–€1,500 + €150–€400/mo |

Your best anchor locally: **the FBiH minimum net wage for 2026 is 1,027 KM** ([FENA](https://www.fena.ba/article/1659228/fbih-government-minimum-wage-in-the-federation-of-bih-set-at-km-1-027-for-2026)). A 350 KM receptionist that never sleeps costs about a third of the cheapest human. Two cautions:

- **Test the voice in Bosnian before you sell it.** ElevenLabs v3 supports the language, but many "Croatian" library voices carry off accents ([json2video](https://json2video.com/ai-voices/elevenlabs/languages/croatian/)). If the voice is weak, lead with WhatsApp chat.
- Generic n8n work on Upwork pays a **median of $23–$30 an hour** ([DevSnipe](https://devsnipe.com/market/rates/n8n-automation); [Upwatcher](https://www.upwatcher.io/market/ai-automation/)). The money is in packaging an outcome for a niche, not in selling hours.

### Websites and vibecoded apps: sell the safe part

AI site builders cost **$10–$30 a month per site**. Freelance Framer builds sell for **$1,800–$4,500 for 1–10 pages** in the US ([Brix](https://brixtemplates.com/blog/how-much-does-a-framer-website-cost)). Locally, a Sarajevo Canton school paid **1,500 KM for a 15-page CMS site** in 2020 ([KS procurement](https://jn.ks.gov.ba/sites/default/files/odluka_o_direktnom_sporazumu_-_web_stranica.pdf)), and a Croatian agency sells packages at **€265 and €995** ([whtop](https://www.whtop.com/plans/webdizajn.biz/132815)). Sell a build fee plus a small monthly "care" fee that covers hosting and edits.

Vibecoded apps pay more, around **$2,000–$15,000 for prompt-to-app builds** ([Forcoda](https://forcoda.com/blog/mvp-development-cost-guide)), but they are risky in a beginner's hands:

- A 2025 flaw in Lovable exposed **170+ production apps** whose databases lacked access rules ([Rafter](https://rafter.so/blog/vibe-coding-security/supabase-rls)).
- An audit of 200 vibe-coded apps found **65.77% of 1,186 vulnerabilities rated Critical or High** ([summary of the arXiv paper](https://note.com/zephel01/n/n83d2bc394e1a?hl=en)).
- Replit's agent once **deleted a production database** ([eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/replit-ai-coding-assistant-failure/)).

The rule: sell marketing sites, landing pages tied to automations, and internal tools that hold **no customer data, logins or payments**, unless a real engineer reviews the work.

Micro-SaaS can be huge. Photo AI reports **~$105K a month in revenue**, self-published ([andrew.ooo](https://andrew.ooo/posts/pieter-levels-photo-ai-solo-founder/)), and Cal AI reached **$30M+ in annual revenue** before MyFitnessPal bought it ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/02/myfitnesspal-has-acquired-cal-ai-the-viral-calorie-app-built-by-teens/)). But both won on distribution, not code. Treat micro-SaaS as a later bet.

### How deals actually close

The repeatable process has four steps ([ciela.ai](https://ciela.ai/blogs/how-to-get-clients-ai-automation-agency)):

1. Pick one niche.
2. Build a working demo for the prospect, ideally a **90-second Loom of an AI agent answering a missed call and booking an appointment**.
3. Send personalized outreach that names one problem.
4. Close with a low-risk pilot.

Generic cold email gets **1–3% replies**. Personalized video reportedly **doubles or triples** that, though this is a vendor claim ([Weezly](https://weezly.com/blog/boost-cold-email-replies-with-loom-videos/)). **Your first two or three clients will almost certainly come from your existing network** (same ciela.ai source). Buyers look for:

- itemized scope
- a support period
- a named contact
- a **30-day exit clause** ([Layer3Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/roi/ai-automation-agency-cost))

Most agencies fail on **sales and proof, not delivery** ([ciela.ai](https://ciela.ai/blogs/ai-automation-agency-reddit-honest-truth)).

## AI ads, AI visibility and content win as a bundle, not alone

### AI UGC ads: sell testing volume, not "better than humans"

AI avatar ads cost about **$5–$30 per video** to make, against **$150–$500 for a human UGC creator** ([Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/ai-ugc-vs-human-ugc-meta-ads-roas-2026); [LaunchPoint](https://www.launchpointhq.com/guides/rates/how-much-do-ecommerce-ugc-creators-charge)). Freelancers resell them at **$50–$200 each**, or **$1,200–$4,000 per batch of 8–15** ([Motionbox](https://motionbox.io/blog/how-much-to-charge-clients-for-ai-ugc-ads)).

The performance evidence comes from vendors. One dataset claims **3.4x average ROAS for AI ads against 3.1x for human ads**, but humans win by **8–15% on YouTube Shorts** ([Lapis](https://landing.trylapis.com/resources/ai-generated-ads-vs-human-ads-performance-data)). Human creators still tend to win in testimonial-heavy niches ([Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/ai-ugc-vs-human-ugc-meta-ads-roas-2026)).

So the honest pitch is "more testable variants per euro, to find winning hooks faster". Sell a **paid test of 10 variants judged on cost per acquisition** against the client's current best ad. Never script an avatar as a real customer: the US FTC rule bans AI-generated fake testimonials, with penalties of up to about **$51,744 per violation** ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/ftc-fake-reviews-testimonials-445209)).

### Creative volume retainers for e-commerce brands

E-commerce creative work is sold monthly. One static-ad retainer delivers **20 concepts × 3 copy variants for $3,200 a month** ([Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/services/122082-Static-Ads-for-DTC-Brands-Monthly)). A freelance strategist charges **$2,800 for 10–15 scripts up to $6,600 for 30–40** ([Influee](https://influee.co/partnership/johnny-kam)). AI cuts production cost so far that a solo operator in Sarajevo can sell this at **$150–$300 per concept** and keep most of it. This is the highest-ceiling foreign lane.

### Meta ads management: bundle it with creatives

Local-business Meta ads management runs **$500–$1,000 a month for setup and monitoring, $1,500–$3,000 for active optimization, or 10–20% of ad spend** ([ClicksGeek](https://clicksgeek.com/facebook-ads-management-cost-per-month/)). Meta made Advantage+ the default and is automating targeting ([Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/meta-pushes-toward-fully-automated-advertising-future-9330538)). The value is therefore moving to **creative supply, offers, lead handling and tracking**. Sell "management + 8–12 AI creatives a month" as one retainer, priced locally at about 400–800 KM plus ad spend, and report cost per lead, not clicks. This matters in BiH, a **Facebook-first market**: Facebook takes **87.5% of social page views** ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/social-media-stats/all/bosnia-and-herzegovina)) and reaches **2.36M users, 68.6% of the population** ([NapoleonCat](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/social-media-users-in-bosnia_and_herzegovina/2026/)).

### Local SEO and "get recommended by ChatGPT"

GEO/AEO means getting a business named in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI answers. **AI referrals are still small, about 0.3–1.1% of website traffic** ([SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/ai-traffic-research-study/); [Conductor](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251113364791/en/Conductor-Unveils-2026-AEO-GEO-Benchmarks-Report-How-AI-Shapes-Brand-Visibility-in-a-Zero-Click-World)), though growing fast. In a study of 10,000 US local businesses, those AI recommended averaged **133.4 Google reviews against 10.7 for those it ignored**, and technical SEO polish showed almost no relationship. That is a correlation, not proof of cause ([Insites](https://insites.com/blog/where-does-chatgpt-get-its-information-about-local-businesses/)).

So "AI visibility" for a local business mostly means:

- a complete Google Business Profile
- a system that generates reviews
- consistent name, address and phone everywhere
- website FAQs
- mentions on local lists and in local media

Google Business Profile work alone sells for **$125–$500 per location per month plus $300–$500 setup** in the US ([Slashpost](https://slashpost.ai/blogs/google-business-profile/google-business-profile-optimization-cost)). ## Faceless brands pay slowly, and only YouTube pays BiH residents directly

### What still works, and what YouTube is deleting

AI-only channels can be huge. Kapwing found **278 AI-only channels among ~15,000 top trending channels**, with **63 billion views and ~$117M a year in estimated ad revenue**. A Bosnian-language channel therefore earns little from ads. English long-form aimed at Tier-1 audiences earns far more.

### Creating content without showing your face

The best no-camera workflow keeps a human performance and swaps the visuals:

1. Record yourself on a phone acting or talking.
2. Run the clip through **Genjutsu Motion Transfer** with reference images of a consistent AI character built in **Soul ID**. Soul ID trains on at least 20 photos in 3–5 minutes ([Higgsfield Soul ID](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/sould-id-best-character-consistency)).
3. Keep your own voice, or use a synthetic one.

Higgsfield's academy describes a full pipeline: portraits, then Soul ID, then scenes, then animation in Kling, then Lipsync Studio ([Higgsfield Academy](https://higgsfield.ai/academy/how-to-use/how-to-create-ai-influencer)). Because the human performance survives, the result also passes YouTube's "original value" test better than fully synthetic clips. Simpler options:

- voiceover over B-roll
- screen recordings
- AI avatars such as HeyGen (Creator plan $29)
- animated characters

### The attention engine

The machine works in five steps:

1. Post **AI-produced short-form content every day**.
2. End each post with a comment keyword ("comment PROMPTS").
3. **ManyChat** DMs the link automatically ([ManyChat guide](https://manychat.com/blog/instagram-quick-automation-comment-to-dm-guide/)). Instagram's rules limit automated follow-ups to the **24-hour messaging window** ([ManyChat rules](https://manychat.com/blog/instagram-dm-automation-rules/)).
4. The buyer checks out with an order bump, then sees a one-click upsell.
5. You build an email list and later make a service offer.

You can also **rent attention** with a Whop clipping campaign, paying clippers about **$1 per 1,000 verified views**, so roughly $1,000 buys a million views ([OpenClip guide](https://openclip.app/guides/whop-clipping-guide)). DM click-through figures of 18–35% come from vendors and are unverified ([Communipass](https://communipass.com/blog/auto-dm-statistics-2026-open-rates-conversion-benchmarks/)).

### Full funnel math: views, clicks, sales, bump, upsell

The standard break-even formula is: **front-end price + (bump price × bump take rate) + (upsell price × upsell take rate)**. The classic example, $27 + $17 × 35% + $67 × 12%, gives **$40.99 average order value**, which is also the most you can pay to acquire a buyer ([LTO Ads](https://ltoads.com/blog/how-to-set-up-self-liquidating-offer-funnel)). Vendors quote bump take rates of **20–40%** ([CartFlows](https://cartflows.com/blog/order-bump/)). Treat that as optimistic: SamCart's own worked example shows about a **9.7% lift in order value**, not the "30%+" in its headline ([SamCart](https://www.samcart.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-order-bumps)). The best independent landing-page benchmark, a **6.6% median conversion**, includes free sign-ups, so paid checkouts from cold social traffic will convert lower ([Search Engine Land on Unbounce](https://searchengineland.com/how-does-your-landing-page-performance-compare-453224)). The rates below are planning assumptions. Replace them with your own data after the first 1,000 visitors.

| Funnel step | Conservative | Realistic | Aggressive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly views | 1,000,000 | 1,000,000 | 3,000,000 |
| Click to link | 0.2% → 2,000 visitors | 0.5% → 5,000 | 0.8% → 24,000 |
| Buy front-end at $17 | 1% → 20 sales = $340 | 2% → 100 sales = $1,700 | 3% → 720 sales = $12,240 |
| Order bump at $9 | 20% take → $36 | 30% → $270 | 35% → $2,268 |
| Upsell at $47 | 5% take → $47 | 8% → $376 | 12% → $4,042 |
| **Gross revenue** | **$423** | **$2,346** | **$18,550** |
| Gumroad fees (10% + $0.50 per transaction) | −$53 | −$289 | −$2,258 |
| **Net revenue** | **$370 (€322 / 630 KM)** | **$2,057 (€1,790 / 3,501 KM)** | **$16,292 (€14,174 / 27,722 KM)** |
| Gross per buyer | $21.15 | $23.46 | $25.76 |
| If the views were bought through clipping at ~$1 per 1,000 | −$1,000 → **loss of $630** | −$1,000 → **profit of $1,057** | −$3,000 → **profit of $13,292** |

Four lessons follow from the table:

1. **Attention is the product.** A million views at weak conversion doesn't even pay for the clipping.
2. In the realistic case, each buyer brings about **$20.57 net**, so paid ads only work below roughly $20 per buyer.
3. **The back end matters most.** If just 2 of the 100 realistic buyers later book a €500 done-for-you service, that adds €1,000, which is more than half again what the product itself netted (€1,790). (The 2% rate is an assumption; no ascension data was found.)
4. Third-party guides say Gumroad's processing fees may come on top, for an effective **12.9% + $0.80**, and that **fees are not refunded** on refunds. Check this in your dashboard ([CheckoutPage](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/gumroad-fees)).

## Bosnia's late start is an edge if you price for two markets

### Why being early pays here

Exact BiH figures for company AI use could not be retrieved, but the region trails badly. **19.95% of EU enterprises used AI in 2025**, while candidate country **Türkiye was at 7.41%** ([Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php/Use_of_artificial_intelligence_in_enterprises); [Cyprus Mail](https://cyprus-mail.com/2025/12/12/cyprus-firms-slow-to-adopt-ai-despite-steady-improvement)). Individuals already use AI informally: **70% of BiH civil servants use AI tools, yet ~95% have had no training** ([UNDP](https://www.undp.org/bs/node/546276)). The visible local AI players, such as 387labs and the SarAI Center, focus on enterprise and development work ([UNSA](https://unsa.ba/node/13277)). No dominant AI marketing agency for small businesses turned up, though the search was limited.

So in most Bosnian towns, the **first person who walks into a café with a stunning 10-second video of its own food** has no competition. A 2024 market report also says **TikTok advertising was not available locally** ([we_know](https://weknow.wecan.net/the-media-market-in-bosnia-and-herzegovina-2024)). That keeps the game on Facebook and Instagram, where you can demonstrate results easily.

The catch is size. BiH has **9.7 SMEs per 1,000 inhabitants against 50.4 in the EU** ([OECD](https://www.oecd-ilibrary.org/development/sme-policy-index-western-balkans-and-turkey-2022_b86496a6-en)), and budgets are small. So the winning sequence is **local proof → diaspora → foreign**.

### Tax and legal basics

Where you register changes your take-home pay a lot.

**Republika Srpska.** A "mali preduzetnik" (small entrepreneur) with **under 100,000 KM in revenue and at most 3 employees pays 2% of collected revenue** as income tax. The minimum is 600 KM a year under 50,000 KM of revenue and 1,200 KM between 50,000 and 100,000 KM ([Novigrad guide](https://www.opstina-novigrad.com/storage/Poreski-i-drugi-troskovi-samostalnih-preduzetnika-u-Republici-Srpskoj-u-2025.-godini.pdf)). Contributions come on top and were not verified.

**Federation of BiH.** Income from foreign clients is self-assessed at **10% income tax plus 4% health contributions**, filed on form AMS-1035 **within five days of receipt** ([Paragraf](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/18062025/18062025-vijest3.html)). Registered entrepreneurs instead pay contributions on fixed monthly bases. In 2023 these were **934 KM for flat-rate crafts and 1,869 KM for book-keeping free professions**, and they rise every year ([Unija](https://unija.com/bs/mjesecne-osnovice-doprinosa-poduzetnici-fbih/)).

**VAT.** Registration becomes mandatory at **100,000 KM** in turnover. Foreign providers of digital services to BiH consumers must charge **17% VAT** ([VATCalc](https://www.vatcalc.com/bosnia-and-herzegovina/bosnia-herzegovina-vat-on-b2c-digital-services/)).

Banks give account turnover data to the tax authorities on request ([RSE](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/freelance-bih-doprinosi-porez/31517840.html)), so undeclared income is visible.

An illustration using the realistic scenario below: 5,375 KM a month is 64,500 KM a year.

- **RS small entrepreneur:** 2% is about **1,290 KM a year**.
- **FBiH 10% + 4% route on gross income:** up to roughly **9,000 KM a year**, before any deductions or contributions that apply to your status.

That is a simplified comparison. **Get a local accountant before your second paying client.**

### The free-sample strategy

Free samples are your best sales tool if you set limits:

- **One free asset per prospect, capped at 30–60 minutes and a few dollars of credits.** Build it from their own public photos so it feels personal.
- Keep any audit to **one or two findings**, or it turns into free consulting ([ClearAudit](https://clearaudit.space/agency-cold-outreach-audit)).
- Make free samples only for your top 20% of prospects. Everyone else gets your portfolio.
- Offer pilots of 14 days, not 60.
- Track the funnel every week: messages sent, replies, calls, closes.

If you have no case studies yet, say plainly that the demo is a prototype ([ciela.ai](https://ciela.ai/blogs/how-to-get-clients-ai-automation-agency)).

**How to close.** Ask five discovery questions: goal, current marketing, budget, decision-maker and deadline. Then:

1. Present all three tiers.
2. Take **50% upfront**.
3. Sign a one-page agreement covering scope, revisions, usage rights, AI disclosure and a 30-day exit clause.
4. Deliver within 72 hours.
5. Ask for a testimonial and two referrals.

Other risks to plan for:

- **Vendor risk.** OpenAI shut down Sora within months of launching its app ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/openai-shuttering-sora-video-generating-service-rcna264989)), so never build on one model. Higgsfield changed its plans at least three times in 2026.
- **Quality and backlash.** Coca-Cola's AI holiday ad drew "AI slop" backlash over trucks with inconsistent wheels ([NBC Bay Area](https://www.nbcbayarea.com/news/national-international/coca-cola-ai-generated-holiday-ad-backlash/3975523/)). Check hands, text and product details on every frame.
- **Platform risk.** Most creator funds are closed to BiH residents.
- **Payment risk.** Few rails work from BiH.
- **Tax visibility.** Banks report account turnover to the tax authorities on request.
- **Legal exposure.** EU AI Act disclosure, the FTC fake-testimonial rule, GDPR for EU clients' data, and an unresearched BiH data-protection law.

Finally, this report rests on **web-search summaries**, because vendor pages and community sites could not be opened during research. **Re-check every price on the vendor's page** before you buy or quote, and treat every income figure as a planning assumption, not a forecast.

## Conclusion

Bosnia's advantage isn't cheap labour. It is a **rare combination**: native Bosnian, Croatian and Serbian plus English or German, the EU's time zone, tools that cost less than a dinner out, local markets where almost no one sells AI services yet, and a two-million-strong diaspora that links you to clients paying three to five times local rates. Whoever pairs AI production with **taste and selling**, the skills tutorials don't teach, captures most of that gap. Tool knowledge goes stale within months; the skills to write a hook, pick the best take, and close a deal compound over time.

The order matters more than the idea. **Start with services, because cash comes in weeks. Add attention assets once services pay the bills. Add products last, once you have an audience.** Most beginners do it in reverse: they buy a course, start a faceless channel, and wait. Make your first video for a real business this week, put a price on it, and let paying clients decide which of these 53 ideas becomes your business.
