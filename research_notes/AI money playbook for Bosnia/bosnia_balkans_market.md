## 1. Business landscape in BiH: businesses, sectors, salaries, marketing prices

### Takeaway
BiH is a small, SME-dominated economy: about 67,000 SMEs (99.6% of firms; 2019–2020 data) and one of the lowest SME densities in Europe. The average net wage is about 1,706 KM (about €872) a month as of June 2026. Local budgets are therefore small. The regional benchmark for social media management runs from about €300 to €1,500 a month, and one documented BiH public-sector website cost 1,500 KM (2020).

### Cited Findings
**Number of businesses / SMEs**
- BiH had 67,009 SMEs (99.6% of enterprises) and 233 large enterprises (0.4%). SMEs employed 374,055 people, or 69.1% of persons employed (2019–2020 estimates, now dated). — [EU SME Fact Sheet BiH 2021](https://neighbourhood-enlargement.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2021-09/bih_-_sme_fact_sheet_2021.pdf)
- OECD (2020 data) puts SME employment at 63.2% (EU 65%) and SME value added at 61% (EU 53%). It counts **9.7 SMEs per 1,000 inhabitants vs 50.4 in the EU**, and notes that BiH improved in 10 of 12 policy areas since 2019. Electronic business registration was among the improvements. — [OECD SME Policy Index WB & Turkey 2022](https://www.oecd-ilibrary.org/development/sme-policy-index-western-balkans-and-turkey-2022_b86496a6-en); [BiH launch minutes](https://t4.oecd.org/south-east-europe/programme/BiH-SMEPI2022-launch-minutes.pdf). The EU and OECD employment figures conflict (63.2% vs 69.1%) because their methods and data years differ.
- The OECD SME Policy Index 2026 (launched in Budva on 12 May 2026) says SMEs generate 66–83% of employment across the Western Balkans and Türkiye, and that their productivity is low relative to the EU. I found no BiH-specific 2026 figures. — [OECD 2026 launch](https://www.oecd.org/en/events/2026/05/regional-high-level-launch-of-the-sme-policy-index-for-the-western-balkans-and-turkiye-2026.html)
- The Agency for Statistics of BiH publishes "Units of the Statistical Business Register". The latest edition found covers 31 Dec 2024 (published 22 Sep 2025), but its totals were not retrievable in this session. — [BHAS SBR 2024](https://bhas.gov.ba/data/Publikacije/Saopstenja/2025/SBR_01_2024_Y1_1_BS.pdf)
- A search summary citing a Sarajevo Canton statistical document gives **124,169 business entities in FBiH at end-2023**. This is low confidence because I could not see the underlying document. FBiH register methodology: [FZS Statistical Business Register](https://fzs.ba/index.php/statisticke-oblasti/poslovne-statistike/statisticki-poslovni-registar/). Live court-register counts by legal form are at [bizreg.pravosudje.ba](https://bizreg.pravosudje.ba/pls/apex/f?p=183:20:1341709613::NO::P20_SEKCIJA_TIP:POMOC).

**Wages (BiH, 2026)**
- Average net wage in legal entities in BiH: **1,706 KM in June 2026**, +0.9% m/m and +8.6% y/y nominal. May 2026: 1,691 KM; April: 1,684 KM; January: 1,644 KM. — [BHAS LAB June 2026](https://bhas.gov.ba/data/Publikacije/Saopstenja/2026/LAB_04_2026_06_1_BS.pdf)
- The H1 2026 average was 1,675 KM, against 1,539 KM in H1 2025. Q1 2026: 1,656 KM; Q2 2026: 1,693 KM. — [BHAS LAB H1 2026](https://bhas.gov.ba/data/Publikacije/Saopstenja/2026/LAB_04_2026_H1_1_HR.pdf); [BHAS LAB Q2 2026](https://bhas.gov.ba/data/Publikacije/Saopstenja/2026/LAB_04_2026_Q2_1_BS.pdf)
- FBiH average net wage, June 2026: 1,714 KM. — [FZS 8.1.6](https://fzs.ba/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/8.1.6.pdf)
- The FBiH minimum net wage for 2026 is set at 1,027 KM. — [FENA, 30 Dec 2025](https://www.fena.ba/article/1659228/fbih-government-minimum-wage-in-the-federation-of-bih-set-at-km-1-027-for-2026)

**Key sectors (only tourism was researched in depth; see section 8)**
- Tourism, Jan–Nov 2025: 1,845,242 arrivals (+0.1% y/y) and 3,846,799 overnights (+0.9%). Foreigners accounted for **70.8% of overnights**. The top foreign source markets were Croatia (13.5%), Serbia (10.8%) and Türkiye (9.9%). — [BHAS TUR Nov 2025](https://bhas.gov.ba/data/Publikacije/Saopstenja/2026/TUR_01_2025_11_1_BS.pdf); [BHAS TUR 02 Nov 2025](https://bhas.gov.ba/data/Publikacije/Saopstenja/2026/TUR_02_2025_11_1_BS.pdf)

**Marketing service prices**
- A Croatian-language regional guide puts social media packages for small businesses at **€300/month at the low end, €700–1,500/month mid-tier, and €1,500–3,000+/month premium**. It is regional (HR/SI), not BiH-specific. — [Kontentino HR guide](https://www.kontentino.com/hr/blog/paketi-za-drustvene-mreze-za-male-tvrtke-vodic-kroz-cijene/)
- The Olympic Committee of BiH ran public calls (2021 and 2025) for an external social-media manager on a 3-month contract. The fee was not visible in the snippet, but the calls show that public bodies outsource this work. — [OK BiH call 2025](https://mail.okbih.ba/assets/files/1738326599-poziv-za-dostavljanje-ponuda-za-usluge-upravljanja-drustvenim-mrezama-ok-bih.pdf)
- A primary school in Hadžići (Sarajevo Canton) awarded a website contract for **1,500 KM excl. VAT** in 2020. Scope: responsive design, CMS, SSL, SEO, up to 15 static pages and social links. — [KS public procurement decision](https://jn.ks.gov.ba/sites/default/files/odluka_o_direktnom_sporazumu_-_web_stranica.pdf)
- Regional website price points: a Croatian agency sells packages at €265.45 and €995 ([whtop listing 1](https://www.whtop.com/plans/webdizajn.biz/132815), [listing 2](https://www.whtop.com/plans/webdizajn.biz/132819)). A Kosovo/Prague freelancer quotes €200 for one-pagers up to €15,000 for complex projects ([svilenkovic.com](https://www.svilenkovic.com/usluge/izrada-sajta-pristina)).

### Inferences
- At about €872/month average net pay, a €300/month retainer equals roughly a third of a local monthly salary. Realistic price points for BiH micro-businesses (cafés, salons, small shops) are probably at or below the low end of the regional guide. Foreign or diaspora clients, larger firms, tourism operators and public bodies are where the €700+ tickets are likely.
- About 9.7 SMEs per 1,000 people means a small addressable pool. Specialising by vertical (tourism, dental, real estate) and selling outside BiH to diaspora, Croatia/EU and DACH clients will matter more than in larger markets.
- Wages rose 8.6% y/y in nominal terms, so purchasing power is growing. Rising labour costs also make an "AI instead of hiring a marketing person" pitch more attractive.

### Gaps
- No OLX.ba or posao.ba listings were retrieved (search budget exhausted). Local price data for social media management, video production, wedding video and websites in KM is still missing. BiH 2025–2026 business-register totals were also not retrievable.
- No data was gathered on the diaspora-services, real estate, construction, auto, dental/medical tourism, beauty, wedding, IT outsourcing or wood/furniture export sectors.
- No Serbian or Croatian salary or price benchmarks were gathered.

## 2. Digital and AI adoption; competitors and community

### Takeaway
BiH is a Facebook-first market. Facebook reaches about 2.2–2.4M people (65–69% of the population), Instagram about 1.48M (43%), and Facebook dominates social traffic (about 88% of social page views). Company-level AI adoption in the Western Balkans lags far behind the EU average of about 20% in 2025. Exact BiH and Serbian percentages were not retrievable. The local AI services scene is thin and mostly B2B or enterprise consultancies, with no visible SMB-focused "AI marketing agency" leader.

### Cited Findings
**Internet & social media**
- DataReportal published a Digital 2026 BiH report. Most of its data reflects October 2025, and DataReportal warns against comparing it with earlier editions because its publication cycle changed. Headline figures were not retrievable in this session. — [DataReportal Digital 2026 BiH](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-bosnia-and-herzegovina)
- Digital 2025: 3.96M cellular mobile connections in early 2025, equal to 126% of the population. — [DataReportal Digital 2025 BiH](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-bosnia-and-herzegovina)
- Digital 2024: 2.67M internet users, an 83.4% penetration rate (start of 2024; dated). — [DataReportal Digital 2024 BiH](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-bosnia-and-herzegovina)
- NapoleonCat (ad-addressable audience) counts **2,363,400 Facebook users in August 2026 (68.6% of the population; 50.3% women)** and 2,229,500 in September 2026 (64.7%). The difference reflects month-to-month revisions. — [NapoleonCat BiH 2026](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/social-media-users-in-bosnia_and_herzegovina/2026/); [NapoleonCat Facebook BiH](https://napoleoncat.com/stats/facebook-users-in-bosnia-and-herzegovina/)
- NapoleonCat counts **Instagram at 1,479,300 users in August 2026 (42.9%)**: 52.6% are women, and 25–34 is the largest age group. Messenger has about 1.73M users and **LinkedIn about 578,000**. — [NapoleonCat Instagram BiH](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/instagram-users-in-bosnia-and-herzegovina/); [NapoleonCat BiH 2026](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/social-media-users-in-bosnia_and_herzegovina/2026/)
- StatCounter's August 2026 share of social media page views in BiH: **Facebook 87.54%**, Instagram 5.27%, YouTube 1.84%. — [StatCounter BiH](https://gs.statcounter.com/social-media-stats/all/bosnia-and-herzegovina)
- StateGlobe (methodology not stated; treat as rough) estimates 2.5M social media users (62% penetration) and about **€45M of social media ad spend**, including about €25M on Instagram in 2026. These figures conflict with NapoleonCat's. — [StateGlobe social](https://data.stateglobe.com/bosnia-and-herzegovina/social-media-users-statistics); [StateGlobe Instagram](https://data.stateglobe.com/bosnia-and-herzegovina/instagram-marketing-statistics)
- A 2024 media-market report says **TikTok advertising was not available locally in BiH** at the time (dated 2024; may have changed). — [we_know: Media Market in BiH 2024](https://weknow.wecan.net/the-media-market-in-bosnia-and-herzegovina-2024)

**AI adoption**
- 19.95% of EU enterprises used AI technologies in 2025. Denmark led at 42.0%, followed by Finland (37.8%) and Sweden (35.0%). — [Eurostat Statistics Explained](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php/Use_of_artificial_intelligence_in_enterprises)
- Candidate country Türkiye reached 7.41% in 2025. — [Cyprus Mail citing Eurostat, Dec 2025](https://cyprus-mail.com/2025/12/12/cyprus-firms-slow-to-adopt-ai-despite-steady-improvement)
- Eurostat's AI publication includes enterprise AI data for Albania, **BiH**, Montenegro and Serbia, and citizen AI-use data for BiH, North Macedonia and Serbia. The exact percentages were not retrievable. — [Western Balkans Info Hub summary](https://westernbalkans-infohub.eu/?p=11106); dataset [isoc_eb_ain2 via DBnomics](https://db.nomics.world/Eurostat/isoc_eb_ain2)
- A UNDP survey in BiH found that **70% of civil servants already use AI tools, but about 95% have never received AI training** (UNDP AI Summit, Sarajevo, Sept 2025). — [UNDP BiH](https://www.undp.org/bs/node/546276)

**Local AI players and community (likely competitors and partners)**
- **387labs**: AI consulting and development (use-case identification and builds). Founder Ned Lomigora also founded Falcon Venture Partners, a VC firm focused on Western Balkan startups. It has engineering in SE Europe and South America and a presence in NYC and Boston. It signed a cooperation agreement on AI with the Faculty of Electrical Engineering (ETF) of the University of Sarajevo, alongside **WiseScore**. — [UNSA panel invitation](https://unsa.ba/node/13277); [ETF UNSA](https://etf.unsa.ba/b0f07b7b-394c-4235-b1f5-5ff527e58515)
- **SarAI Center** (Sarajevo): aims to accelerate enterprise AI adoption and is launching a GPU & AI Center in BiH (2025 listing). — [b2match CL4 Digital 2025](https://www.b2match.com/e/cl4-digital-2025/participations/519051)
- **AI Project Hub** was presented in Sarajevo in January 2026 by the SMART Center for Lifelong Learning and Excellence, the BiH Ministry of Civil Affairs and WinCode Predictive Systems. It uses AI for EU-project preparation. — [FENA, 20 Jan 2026](https://fena.ba/article/1661862/ai-project-hub-presented-in-sarajevo-eu-projects-preparation-with-the-support-of-ai)
- Events and community: the UNDP AI Summit in Sarajevo (Sept 2025) ([UNDP](https://www.undp.org/bs/node/546276)); a UNSA/ETF panel on "Potential for development and application of AI in BiH" ([UNSA](https://unsa.ba/node/13277)); and student AI hackathons such as the EESTech Challenge ([ETF UNSA](https://www.etf.unsa.ba/en)).

### Inferences
- **Lead with Facebook and Instagram (Meta ads) in BiH.** TikTok and YouTube matter for reaching young audiences and for faceless content, but they are a small share of measured social traffic. TikTok's local ad availability was limited as of 2024.
- About 578k LinkedIn users makes LinkedIn viable for B2B outreach within BiH, though at small scale.
- Low company AI adoption combined with high informal individual use (UNDP's 70%/95% finding for civil servants) points to demand for **training and done-for-you implementation**. AI workshops for SMEs and public bodies are a plausible entry product.
- The visible AI players are enterprise and dev consultancies. I found no evidence of a dominant SMB-focused AI marketing or video-ad agency in BiH, though this is not conclusive given the limited search.

### Gaps
- Exact Eurostat 2025 enterprise AI percentages for BiH, Serbia and Montenegro.
- DataReportal 2026 headline numbers, including TikTok and YouTube ad reach, which were not retrievable.
- AI agencies in Serbia, Croatia and North Macedonia, and BiH digital agencies already selling AI video or chatbots (no searches possible).
- Local communities such as GDG Sarajevo and the Sarajevo/Banja Luka meetup scene are mentioned in summaries but unverified.

## 3. Language and tools: AI quality in Bosnian/Croatian/Serbian

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin and partly conflicting. **Croatian is the best-supported BCMS variant** in ElevenLabs dubbing; Serbian and Bosnian appear on ElevenLabs text-to-speech pages but not on the 29–30-language dubbing list cited by third parties. In practice Croatian voices are often used for all BCMS audiences, which is an inference. HeyGen, ChatGPT/Claude and Whisper could not be researched.

### Cited Findings
- A third-party guide to ElevenLabs Dubbing lists 30 supported languages, including **Croatian but not Serbian or Bosnian**. Another source describes the dubbing model as covering 29 languages, so the counts conflict. — [Scenario help: ElevenLabs Dubbing](https://help.scenario.com/articles/1136446562-elevenlabs-dubbing-the-essentials); [Segmind ElevenLabs Dubbing](https://www.segmind.com/models/dubbing)
- An older third-party listing of ElevenLabs languages includes Croatian, not Serbian or Bosnian. — [SoftwareSuggest](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/elevenlabs)
- ElevenLabs has its own text-to-speech landing pages for **Croatian, Serbian (claims it adapts to regional Serbian accents) and Bosnian**. These cover TTS, not necessarily dubbing. — [ElevenLabs Croatian](https://elevenlabs.io/ja/text-to-speech/croatian); [ElevenLabs Serbian](https://elevenlabs.io/ko/text-to-speech/serbian); [ElevenLabs Bosnian](https://elevenlabs.io/text-to-speech/bosnian)
- Competitor VideoDubber markets Bosnian, Serbian and Croatian TTS (marketing pages only). — [VideoDubber Bosnian](https://videodubber.ai/text-to-speech/bosnian/)

### Inferences
- Bosnian, Croatian, Serbian and Montenegrin are mutually intelligible. A Croatian voice or dub is usable across BiH and Serbia, but the accent and vocabulary (ijekavian/ekavian, local words) will sound "foreign" in some markets. Ads aimed at Serbia (ekavian) or Bosnia should be checked by a native speaker.
- **Local-language quality control is a selling point.** A native BCMS speaker who fixes AI scripts, pronunciations and subtitles adds value that foreign AI agencies cannot easily match.

### Gaps
- HeyGen video translation and lip-sync language list for Bosnian/Serbian/Croatian (search budget exhausted).
- ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini quality in BCMS: no benchmarks found or searched.
- Whisper transcription accuracy. Unverified background knowledge, to confirm: OpenAI's Whisper lists Bosnian (bs), Croatian (hr) and Serbian (sr) as separate languages.
- Whether ElevenLabs v3 or later (2025–2026) expanded dubbing to Serbian or Bosnian. Check the current Dubbing Studio language selector.

## 4. Diaspora as a market

### Takeaway
The BiH Ministry for Human Rights and Refugees estimates **about 1.67M BiH-born people in 51 countries, and about 2M including descendants**. That is more than half of BiH's resident population. The largest communities are in Germany, Austria, the USA, Slovenia, Switzerland and Sweden. These figures are old (mid-2010s interviews) and likely undercount later emigration.

### Cited Findings
- 1,671,000 people born in BiH live in 51 countries, and with second and third generations the total approaches 2 million. Approximate counts by country: **Germany about 160,000; Austria about 150,000; USA about 132,000; Slovenia about 97,000; Switzerland about 57,000; Sweden about 57,000**. Most have taken citizenship of their host countries. The ministry has no complete statistics of its own and relies on foreign statistical offices and Eurostat. — [RSE interview with Deputy Minister Predrag Jović](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/iseljenici-bih-strategija-/28699444.html); [RSE interview, Haris Halilović](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/intervju-haris-halilovic-dijaspora-bih/28474078.html). **Date flag:** the article IDs suggest about 2017. Treat these as dated estimates.
- UNDP runs a diaspora-and-development programme with success stories of diaspora investment in BiH. — [UNDP: Iseljeništvo i razvoj BiH](https://www.undp.org/bs/node/90946)
- Remittance and money-transfer providers actively target BiH as a receiving country: Xoom/PayPal offers bank deposit, cash pickup and debit card ([Xoom BiH](https://partner.xoom.com/bosnia-and-herzegovina)), and Paysend added BiH as a receiving country ([Paysend](https://paysend.com/de-ie/blog/send-money-albania-bosnia-paysend)).

### Inferences
- The diaspora has DACH, Nordic and US incomes, BCMS language ties and frequent summer and holiday travel home. That makes it a strong audience for faceless content (nostalgia, food, humour, football, "home" news) and a buyer pool for BiH real estate, dental work, weddings, events and gifts. It is also a natural first market for German-speaking BiH freelancers.
- The real diaspora is almost certainly larger than these figures, given heavy emigration since about 2015 (especially to Germany and Austria). The report should cite current Destatis/Statistik Austria numbers once retrieved.

### Gaps
- Current (2024–2026) official counts of BiH, Serbian and Croatian citizens and BiH-born residents in Germany, Austria, Switzerland and Scandinavia.
- Remittance volumes (World Bank) as a share of GDP.
- Size of the BCMS-language audience on YouTube and TikTok. No data retrieved.

## 5. Selling to foreign clients from BiH

### Takeaway
Western Balkan freelancers are an established presence on Upwork: about 23k WB6 freelancers in 2022, and over 100k regional freelancers across platforms by 2021. Senior BiH developers command about $35–44/hour via vetted marketplaces. Growth in BiH has been slower than its neighbours', and women make up only about 30% of BiH platform workers.

### Cited Findings
- Lemon.io (vetted marketplace, own dataset of 67 senior contracts): BiH **senior developers' median rate is $44/hr, mid-level $35/hr**. — [Lemon.io BiH rate calculator 2026](https://lemon.io/rate-calculator/bosnia-and-herzegovina/)
- Stealth Agents, citing Paylab and levels.fyi (2025), puts a mid-level software developer in BiH at about **€18–38/hr** (salary-derived; conflicts with the Lemon.io range). — [Stealth Agents BiH BPO](https://stealthagents.com/research/bosnia-and-herzegovina-bpo)
- UNDP analysed about **23,000 WB6 freelancers registered on Upwork** (June 2022) and noted the true total is higher because many work off-platform. — [UNDP Digital Work – Upwork analysis](https://www.undp.org/node/324636)
- Gigmetar (May 2021): Upwork, Freelancer and Guru together had **over 100,000 freelancers from the region**, and Upwork was the top platform in 7 of 9 countries, including BiH. A later Gigmetar release says BiH recorded the smallest increase (14.4%) and the widest gender gap, with **women at 29.8%**. — [Gigmetar: Gig workers in the region](https://gigmetar.repeople.rs/?p=3909); [Gigmetar region](https://gigmetar.repeople.rs/en/region4-2/)
- The regional online freelance workforce contracted slightly, by 0.7% between July 2024 and May 2025, with Albania declining most. — [Balkanweb, 2025](https://www.balkanweb.com/en/There-are-no-more-online-jobs-in-Albania-with-the-largest-drop-in-the-number-of-freelance-workers/)
- Upwork's BiH hiring pages say rates vary with experience, location and market conditions, and publish no BiH average. — [Upwork BA](https://www.upwork.com/hire/html-developers/ba/)

### Inferences
- BiH shares the CET time zone with all of the EU and the DACH region. With the German-speaking diaspora and good English among young people, DACH SMEs and diaspora-owned businesses are the highest-value outbound target. Rates of $25–50/hr would be about 3–6x local hourly wage levels while still well below Western agency rates. This is an inference from the $35–44 developer medians and the €872 average monthly net wage.
- The 2024–2025 contraction in platform freelancing suggests that **direct outreach (LinkedIn, cold email, diaspora networks)** may matter more than relying only on Upwork.

### Gaps
- Upwork rates specifically for AI video, automation and marketing freelancers from BiH or Serbia.
- How Balkan agencies win foreign clients in practice (case studies). Not searched.
- Number of active (not registered) BiH freelancers in 2025–2026.

## 6. Legal and tax setup for a freelancer in BiH; payments infrastructure

### Takeaway
Republika Srpska has the simplest regime: a **"small entrepreneur" (mali preduzetnik) pays 2% of collected revenue** (minimum 600 or 1,200 KM a year) if revenue is under 100,000 KM and there are no more than 3 employees. FBiH contributions are paid on a fixed monthly base set each year. In 2023 that base was 934 KM for flat-rate crafts and 1,869 KM for book-keeping "free professions"; 2026 bases were not retrievable. VAT registration starts at 100,000 KM turnover. **Stripe cannot be used as a normal merchant account from BiH**; BiH is "only available for cross-border payouts accounts". PayPal receiving in BiH could not be confirmed.

### Cited Findings
**Republika Srpska**
- Entrepreneurs with **under 100,000 KM (≈€51,100) annual revenue and up to 3 employees can be "mali preduzetnici" and pay income tax of 2% of collected revenue**. The minimum is 600 KM/year if revenue is under 50,000 KM and 1,200 KM/year if revenue is 50,000–100,000 KM. All others are "veliki preduzetnici" and pay **10% on revenue minus expenses**. — [Novigrad municipality guide: tax costs of entrepreneurs in RS, 2025](https://www.opstina-novigrad.com/storage/Poreski-i-drugi-troskovi-samostalnih-preduzetnika-u-Republici-Srpskoj-u-2025.-godini.pdf)
- The small-entrepreneur threshold was raised from 50,000 KM to 100,000 KM in late 2024 to match the mandatory VAT (indirect tax) threshold and the FBiH flat-rate threshold. — [Novigrad 2025 guide](https://www.opstina-novigrad.com/storage/Poreski-i-drugi-troskovi-samostalnih-preduzetnika-u-Republici-Srpskoj-u-2025.-godini.pdf); consolidated law (amendments through 110/2024): [RS Income Tax Law](https://advokat-prnjavorac.com:8443/zakoni/Zakon-o-porezu-na-dohodak-Republike-Srpske.pdf)
- In RS, directors on a management contract pay **contributions of 31% on gross pay**. I found no 2026 entrepreneur-specific RS contribution base. — [Novigrad guide: tax costs of a DOO in RS, 2025](https://www.opstina-novigrad.com/storage/Poreski-i-drugi-troskovi-DOO-u-Republici-Srpskoj-u-2025.-godini.pdf)

**Federation of BiH**
- The Federal Minister of Finance publishes monthly contribution bases for entrepreneurs for the following year by 31 December. Each base is the FBiH average wage for January–September multiplied by set coefficients, so it rises with wages. — [Zakon o doprinosima FBiH (Paragraf)](https://www.paragraf.ba/propisi/fbih/zakon-o-doprinosima.html)
- Monthly bases in 2023: **flat-rate (paušal) crafts 934 KM; individual trader 493 KM; "slobodna zanimanja" (free professions keeping books) 1,869 KM**. The 2022 flat-rate craft base was 841 KM. In 2025 bases rose again; one source cites an extra 81.25 KM/month in contributions for free professions. — [Unija: monthly contribution bases for FBiH entrepreneurs](https://unija.com/bs/mjesecne-osnovice-doprinosa-poduzetnici-fbih/); [Unija (2)](https://unija.com/bs/mjesecne-osnovice-doprinosa-poduzetnici-fbih-2/)
- A new FBiH Income Tax Law proposal was in parliamentary procedure in 2025, so rules may have changed. Verify current law. — [Parliament FBiH: Draft Law on Income Tax (2025)](https://parlamentfbih.gov.ba/v2/userfiles/file/Materijali%20u%20proceduri_2025/PRIJEDLOG%20ZAKONA%20porezu%20na%20dohodak-%20B%20(1).pdf)

**Payments**
- Stripe's payout documentation lists BiH (IBAN + SWIFT/BIC) but states **"Bosnia and Herzegovina is only available for cross-border payouts accounts"**. That means a BiH person can receive payouts from a platform such as a US Stripe Connect marketplace, but cannot open a standard Stripe account to charge customers. — [Stripe payouts docs](https://docs.stripe.com/payouts.md); [Stripe cross-border payouts](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/cross-border-payouts)
- Elgato's Stripe Connect country list (accurate as of 1 April 2026) includes BiH. Mercor also lists BiH as Stripe-supported for its payouts. — [Elgato](https://docs.elgato.com/monetization/stripe); [Mercor](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/policies/supported-countries)
- Fees: the standard cross-border payout fee is 0.25%. Stripe's Global Payouts pricing table lists **1.00% for BiH** (a different product). — [Stripe Global Payouts pricing](https://edge-docs.stripe.com/global-payouts/pricing)
- Bandcamp's Stripe-payout country list does not include BiH, while Behance lists BiH for PayPal only. Platform support varies. — [Bandcamp](https://get.bandcamp.help/hc/en-us/articles/32854280223511); [Behance](https://help.behance.net/hc/en-us/articles/13356532352667-FAQ-Who-can-sell-assets-on-Behance)
- PayPal's Receive Funds agreement names BiH, but the page was last updated in 2014. PayPal says that in some countries services are cross-border only, or that receiving requires a licensed local partner. Current receive and withdrawal capability for BiH accounts was **not confirmed**. — [PayPal recpymt agreement](https://www.paypal.com/legalhub/recpymt-full); [PayPal FAQ558](https://www.paypal.com/selfhelp/article/FAQ558)
- Payout services report that payouts to BiH Visa/Mastercard bank cards work in USD and EUR. — [Solar Staff: tested BiH bank cards](https://help.solarstaff.com/en/articles/7109126-tested-bank-cards-of-bosnia-and-herzegovina)

**Serbia (context only)**
- Serbia has a separate flat-rate ("paušalac") regime, with 2026 tax assessments issued by the Tax Administration. Do not mix it up with RS (Republika Srpska) rules. — [N1 Serbia](https://n1info.rs/biznis/preduzetnicima-pausalcima-stizu-resenja-o-razrezanom-porezu-za-2026/)

### Inferences
- Example for an RS small entrepreneur: at 58,000 KM (≈€30k) revenue, 2% income tax is about 1,160 KM, so the 1,200 KM (≈€614) annual minimum applies. Contributions come on top and were not verified. For a freelancer selling abroad this is one of the lightest regimes in the region, so **registered address/entity choice (RS vs FBiH) materially affects take-home pay**.
- Without a native Stripe account, a BiH seller of digital products or subscriptions needs a workaround: a merchant-of-record platform (Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Paddle, Polar, etc.; BiH eligibility unverified), invoicing by bank transfer in EUR, or an EU-registered company. Invoicing B2B clients by SWIFT/IBAN in EUR is the low-friction default.

### Gaps
- 2026 FBiH contribution bases and the current FBiH flat-rate (paušal) tax amount; 2026 RS entrepreneur contribution base and rates.
- **Unverified background knowledge to confirm** (no sources retrieved in this session): BiH VAT is a single 17% rate; the KM is pegged to the euro at 1.95583 under a currency board; FBiH employee contributions have historically totalled 41.5% of gross and FBiH income tax is 10%; RS contributions total 31% (PIO 18.5%, health 10.2%, unemployment 0.6%, child protection 1.7%).
- VAT treatment of services exported to EU and US clients (place of supply), and the rules for opening FX (EUR) accounts at BiH banks.
- Whether Payoneer, Wise, Lemon Squeezy, Paddle and Gumroad support BiH residents. Not searched.

## 7. Faceless and attention brands with Balkan potential

### Takeaway
Demand signals exist. Yugo-nostalgia content on Instagram has turned into merchandise businesses (e.g., "Made in Yugoslavia"), Balkan meme culture is large and cross-border, and BiH's qualification for the **2026 FIFA World Cup** created a national football content moment. However, I could not retrieve follower counts for the largest Balkan meme, history, food, kids or AI-education pages, so the "gap" analysis is unproven.

### Cited Findings
- **"Made in Yugoslavia"**: a Slovenian Instagram account started by Špela Golcer to document her collection of Yugoslav objects. It quickly became popular and turned into a merchandise brand (a proof point for "nostalgia page → product"). — [Ara: Yugonostalgia among young people](https://es.ara.cat/internacional/europa/yugonostalgia-jovenes-anoran-pais-no-vivieron_130_5569144.html)
- Academic work describes intergenerational "Jugonostalgija" on Instagram, where users recreate a shared Yugoslav cultural sphere through common references. — [Euroacademia: Yugoslavia on Instagram](https://euroacademia.eu/presentation/yugoslavia-on-instagram-the-reconstruction-of-a-collective-identity-from-the-past/); background: [Wikipedia: Yugo-nostalgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yugo-nostalgia)
- Balkan memes, captioned images bantering between neighbouring nations, are a recognised genre. The main Reddit hub, r/BalkanPeopleInternet, was banned in June 2020 for promoting hate, which shows the moderation risk of ethnic-banter content. — [meme.com: Balkan Memes](https://meme.com/memes/balkan-memes/md)
- Serbian celebrity/estrada Instagram account "Droljana" has about 314K followers (third-party estimate, August 2026). — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/droljana/)
- **BiH qualified for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.** Head coach Sergej Barbarez named the official squad on 11 May 2026. — [beIN Sports](https://www.beinsports.com/en-us/soccer/fifa-world-cup-2026/articles/sergej-barbarez-s-official-bosnia-and-herzegovina-squad-for-the-2026-fifa-world-cup-2026-05-11); [NBC Boston on Esmir Bajraktarević](https://www.nbcboston.com/news/sports/soccer/esmir-bajraktarevic-revolution-fire-bosnia-herzegovina-world-cup/)
- Influencer directories for BiH TikTok and YouTube exist; no counts were captured. — [Modash BiH TikTok](https://www.modash.io/find-influencers/tiktok/bosnia-and-herzegovina); [HypeTrace BiH TikTok](https://hypetrace.com/top-tiktok-influencers-in-bosnia-and-herzegovina/)
- Political figures dominate Balkan Instagram rankings: Vučić leads in the Balkans. — [Vreme](https://vreme.com/drustvo/zelenski-je-kralj-instagrama-vucic-lider-na-balkanu/)

### Inferences
- The highest-potential faceless formats are those that **cross borders inside the BCMS language area** (BiH, Serbia, Croatia, Montenegro, plus about 2M+ BiH diaspora and larger Serbian and Croatian diasporas): Yugo-nostalgia and history, food and recipes (grandma-style Balkan dishes), football (World Cup 2026 momentum, diaspora players), and Balkan humour. Monetisation paths include merch (the Made in Yugoslavia model), affiliate links, sponsored posts from local brands, and AI-education info-products.
- Ethnic and national banter memes carry real deplatforming and reputational risk (the subreddit ban). Nostalgia, food and football content is safer.
- An "AI na našem jeziku" education brand is plausible, given high informal AI use and almost no training (the UNDP civil-servant data), but **no evidence was gathered on whether such a brand already exists**.

### Gaps
- Follower counts for the biggest Balkan meme, history, Yugo-nostalgia, football, food (e.g., Coolinarika, Balkan Lunchbox), tourism, news-aggregator and kids pages, from Social Blade and similar tools. Search budget exhausted. Unverified leads to check: Klix.ba (largest BiH portal), Coolinarika (Croatian recipe platform owned by Podravka), and Serbian children's song channels.
- Viral AI turbo-folk parodies and AI Balkan music: not researched.
- Existing BCMS-language AI education channels and courses, and their audience size: not researched. The gap claim is unverified.

## 8. Opportunities specific to Bosnia: tourism, diaspora, weddings, public bodies, grants

### Takeaway
Tourism is foreign-driven (71% of overnights) but **stagnated in 2025** (+0.1% arrivals; July overnights −8.6% y/y). That gives tourism businesses a reason to invest in better video and marketing. EU-linked money for SME digitalisation exists, notably the **EBRD/EU "Go Digital in the Western Balkans"** programme (€350M loans + €27.6M EU grants/TA, with a **10% grant incentive** on loans), which grew out of a BiH pilot. Most grant calls are loan-linked or periodic, so they cannot be counted on as direct payment for AI services.

### Cited Findings
**Tourism**
- Jan–Nov 2025: 1,845,242 arrivals (+0.1%), 3,846,799 overnights (+0.9%), foreign share of overnights 70.8% (+0.7% y/y). Top markets: Croatia 13.5%, Serbia 10.8%, Türkiye 9.9%. — [BHAS TUR Nov 2025](https://bhas.gov.ba/data/Publikacije/Saopstenja/2026/TUR_01_2025_11_1_BS.pdf); [BHAS TUR 02 Nov 2025](https://bhas.gov.ba/data/Publikacije/Saopstenja/2026/TUR_02_2025_11_1_BS.pdf)
- Q1 2025 foreign overnights rose 6.9% y/y, but **July 2025 overnights (480,501) were 8.6% lower than July 2024**, and November foreign overnights fell 2.6% y/y. — [BHAS TUR Q1 2025](https://bhas.gov.ba/data/Publikacije/Saopstenja/2025/TUR_01_2025_03_1_HR.pdf); [BHAS TUR July 2025](https://bhas.gov.ba/data/Publikacije/Saopstenja/2025/TUR_01_2025_07_1_HR.pdf)

**Grants and programmes that could fund digitalisation**
- **EBRD + EU "Go Digital in the Western Balkans"** (presented in Sarajevo, 10 April 2025): up to **€350M in loans** through 10+ partner banks and leasing companies, plus **€27.6M in EU grant incentives and technical support**. Borrowers receive **grant incentives of 10% of the loan**, with emphasis on women-led SMEs. It builds on a **€61.5M BiH pilot (since 2023) that supported about 200 local companies**, and aims to reach about 750 SMEs regionally. — [EEAS/EU Delegation BiH](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/bosnia-and-herzegovina/eu-and-ebrd-launch-%E2%82%AC377-million-sme-digitalisation-programme-western-balkans_en); [FENA, 10 Apr 2025](https://fena.ba/article/1622546/ebrd-presents-go-digital-in-the-western-balkans-program-worth-377-million-eur); [Reuters via Global Banking & Finance](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/EBRD-BALKANS-DIGITALISATION-5da1b075-8249-4891-bb86-4df66ff82eac/); [WeBalkans](https://webalkans.eu/en/?p=1977486)
- **EU4BusinessRecovery** (EU + Germany): a public call worth 1M KM for digital transformation investments in primary agricultural production (date not shown; check status). — [KFBiH](https://www.kfbih.com/en/?p=32200)
- **EU4DigitalSME**: a 2023 grant call with a €700,000 budget (deadline 10 March 2023; closed) and a 2022 call to set up three Digital Innovation Hubs to support SME digital transformation. — [EU4DigitalSME Guidelines](https://unibl.org/uploads/files/strane/opste_novosti/1.%20EU4DigitalSME_Guidelines%20for%20Grant%20Applicants.pdf); [EEAS DIH call](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/bosnia-and-herzegovina/public-call-digital-innovation-hubs-bih-new-era-digitalisation-small-and-medium-size-enterprises_en)
- **EU4Business** (2019): a €3M call for SME innovation, cooperation and digitisation (closed; shows the recurring pattern of calls). — [FENA](https://fena.ba/article/1094381/eu4business-project-public-call-three-million-euros-for-smes-in-bih)
- **EU4 Digital Transformation (EU4DT)**, co-financed by the EU and BMZ and implemented by GIZ: a call for individual experts was posted on 5 Aug 2026 (consultancy, not SME grants; a possible contracting channel for AI experts). — [MojPosao.ba](https://www.mojposao.ba/savjet/7c9d4ed0-909d-11f1-95dc-42010a9c0018/call-for-expressions-of-interest)
- Local calls for SME innovation and digitalisation projects appear on Akta.ba, and the RS Chamber of Commerce keeps a current list of public calls. — [Akta.ba call](https://www.akta.ba/tenderi/javni-pozivi/2134646/javni-poziv-za-prijedlog-projekta-za-jacanje-inovacija-i-digitalizacije-u-malim-i-srednjim-preduzeci); [Komora RS – Aktuelni javni pozivi](https://infobiz.komorars.ba/?p=259956)
- Public bodies already buy outsourced digital and social media services, e.g., the Olympic Committee of BiH ([OK BiH call](https://mail.okbih.ba/assets/files/1738326599-poziv-za-dostavljanje-ponuda-za-usluge-upravljanja-drustvenim-mrezama-ok-bih.pdf)) and a school in Sarajevo Canton ([KS procurement](https://jn.ks.gov.ba/sites/default/files/odluka_o_direktnom_sporazumu_-_web_stranica.pdf)). 70% of civil servants use AI but 95% are untrained ([UNDP](https://www.undp.org/bs/node/546276)).

### Inferences
- **Tourism video packages**: flat arrivals despite a 71% foreign share suggest operators need better international-facing content. AI-assisted multilingual short video for German, Turkish and English audiences fits hotels, tours and restaurants in Sarajevo, Mostar and Neum. Türkiye, Croatia and Serbia are the top source markets, so Turkish-language ad variants are a concrete differentiator.
- **Public sector and tourist boards**: they already procure social media and website services through simple direct agreements (e.g., 1,500 KM) and have an untrained, AI-using workforce. AI training workshops and content retainers for municipalities and tourist boards fit procurement thresholds.
- **Grant-adjacent selling**: the EBRD 10% incentive is attached to loans, so the realistic play is partnering with firms that are digitising (software, e-commerce, automation) and positioning AI implementation as an eligible digital investment. Eligibility rules were not verified.
- **Diaspora real estate, weddings and events, dental tourism**: these are logical niches given the 2M diaspora and summer homecoming season. No direct demand data was gathered.

- **USAID programmes**: not researched. Unverified background: USAID was largely dismantled by the US government in 2025, so USAID-funded BiH programmes (e.g., tourism and SME projects) should be assumed closed unless confirmed.
