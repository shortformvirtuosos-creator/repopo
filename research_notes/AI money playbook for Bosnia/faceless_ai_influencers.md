## 1. Which faceless and AI-generated formats work in 2025–2026, and which are saturated?

### Takeaway
Fully AI-generated channels can still reach huge audiences and earn millions in estimated ad revenue. The top performers are animal/character "slop", kids and storytelling, and "wisdom" or motivational Shorts. But YouTube began terminating the most egregious of these channels in late 2025, and the copycat "AI vlog" formats (for example Bigfoot vlogs) are now mostly sold as courses. That is a classic sign of saturation. Ad money depends far more on niche and audience geography than on format: finance and education pay the most, kids content and Shorts pay the least.

### Cited Findings
**Scale and earnings of AI-only channels (Kapwing "AI Slop Report", Nov/Dec 2025)**
- Kapwing looked at about 15,000 channels (the top 100 trending channels in each country). It found 278 that posted only AI-generated content. Together those channels had more than 63 billion views and 221 million subscribers, and an estimated ~$117M a year in ad revenue. The revenue figures are Social Blade model estimates, not actual payouts. — [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/); [BetaNews, 3 Dec 2025](https://betanews.com/2025/12/03/study-finds-ai-slop-videos-spreading-fast-across-youtube-and-theres-a-lot-of-money-being-made/)
- Top AI-only channels by views:
  - **Bandar Apna Dost** (India): 2.07B views, about $4.25M/yr estimated.
  - **Three Minutes Wisdom** (South Korea): 2.02B views, about $4.04M/yr estimated. Four South Korean channels were in the global top 10 by estimated earnings.
  - **Pouty Frenchie** (Singapore): about 2B views, close to $4M/yr estimated.
  - **Cuentos Facinantes** (US, Spanish-language storytelling) had the most subscribers (5.95M) but was not among the top earners.
  - — [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/india-youtube-bandar-apna-dost-channel-global-ai-video-charts-slop-content-125123100396_1.html)
- On a fresh YouTube account, "brainrot" made up about 33% of the first 500 Shorts served, and AI slop specifically about 21%. — [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/one-in-five-youtube-shorts-shown-to-new-users-is-ai-generated-slop-study-finds/); [The Standard HK](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/innovation/article/320275/Over-21-percent-videos-shown-to-new-YouTube-users-are-AI-slop-study-finds); [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/ai-generated-content-accounts-for-third-of-youtube-shorts-feed-kapwing-research-shows/)
- After the study, YouTube reportedly removed 16 of the channels, whose combined estimated earnings were $9,785,600 a year (Kapwing, using Social Blade midpoints). Two of the five most-subscribed AI slop channels in the world were deleted. — [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-removes-major-ai-slop-channels-with-combined-9-8m-in-estimated-annual-revenue/)

**Formats that were killed: AI fake movie trailers (Dec 2025)**
- In December 2025 YouTube permanently terminated **Screen Culture** and **KH Studio**, which made AI fake movie trailers. YouTube said that after being readmitted to YPP they "reverted to clear violations of our spam and misleading metadata policies". The history:
  1. The channels were first demonetized after a Deadline investigation.
  2. They regained monetization by adding "fan trailer", "parody" or "concept trailer" to titles.
  3. They later dropped those labels and were terminated.
  - — [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/?p=7915970); [OECD.AI incident log](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2025-12-18-1864); [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-terminates-ai-generated-fake-movie-trailer-channels/); [Gigazine](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20251223-youtube-shuts-down-ai-fake-movie-trailers-channels)
- Screen Culture's founder said a team of editors kept re-editing videos "to exploit YouTube's algorithm". — [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/?p=7915970)

**AI character vlogs (Veo 3 "Bigfoot vlog" trend, mid-2025)**
- The only search results with numbers were Gumroad sales pages selling "Bigfoot AI vlog" blueprints. Their claims are self-reported and not verified:
  - about 13–15M views and 330K+ followers in 3 days (guide updated 14 June 2025);
  - "20M+ views, 414,000 shares… over $3000 in 8 days";
  - "$5,000 from TikTok monetization and $2,000 from YouTube in the first month";
  - "30M+ views in 10 days".
  - The claims contradict each other, and the sellers profit from inflating them. — [bigfootsasquatch Gumroad](https://bigfootsasquatch.gumroad.com/l/bigfootvlogs); [BigfootBoyz Gumroad](https://bigfootboyz.gumroad.com/l/aivlogs); [bigfootpaulvlogs Gumroad](https://bigfootpaulvlogs.gumroad.com/l/pkqvt); [elprota Gumroad](https://elprota.gumroad.com/l/oqrjho)

**What different niches pay on ad revenue (RPM benchmarks for 2026)**
- These ranges were compiled across AIR Media-Tech, vidIQ and OutlierKit. I could not tie each number to exactly one of those pages.
  - Finance/investing: roughly $10–25 RPM, with top channels at $29+. Other sources say $15–30 or $20–40.
  - Motivation/self-improvement: about $6–12 RPM, because the audience skews younger.
  - History/documentary: about $5–12 RPM, because the audience is geographically mixed.
  - — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/which-youtube-niche-makes-the-most-money-in-2026-ranked-by-real-rpm-and-cpm); [vidIQ](https://www.vidiq.com/blog/post/most-profitable-youtube-niches); [OutlierKit benchmarks](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-rpm-benchmarks/); [OutlierKit niches](https://outlierkit.com/blog/most-profitable-youtube-niches)
- AIR Media-Tech's study used YouTube Studio data from 300 channels; four niches had fewer than 10 channels each. It found:
  - **Education** had the highest median RPM at $10.22. That is more than 4× the median across all niches and about 30× what kids content earns.
  - **Shorts** pay $0.02–$1.48 RPM depending on niche (274 channels), which is roughly 3–14% of long-form.
  - — [AIR Media-Tech niches](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/which-youtube-niche-makes-the-most-money-in-2026-ranked-by-real-rpm-and-cpm); [AIR Media-Tech Shorts](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/what-is-youtube-shorts-rpm-in-your-niche-in-2026); [AIR Media-Tech 300 channels](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/how-much-does-youtube-really-pay-in-2026-real-rpm-data-from-300-channels)
- Channels whose audience is mainly in emerging markets (India, Southeast Asia, Latin America) typically see RPMs 60–80% below US-baseline rates. — [OutlierKit benchmarks](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-rpm-benchmarks/)
- OutlierKit argues that a channel's position inside a niche moves earnings more than the niche choice itself. — [OutlierKit niches](https://outlierkit.com/blog/most-profitable-youtube-niches)

### Inferences
- **Formats that have shown scale:**
  - AI animal/character Shorts (Pouty Frenchie, Bandar Apna Dost)
  - wisdom/motivational Shorts (Three Minutes Wisdom)
  - AI storytelling in big languages (Cuentos Facinantes)
- **Weaker economics:** these Shorts-heavy formats earn little per view ($0.02–$1.48 RPM). They only work at billions of views, and they are exactly what YouTube's "inauthentic content" enforcement targets.
- **Kids content:** AIR's "30× less than education" implies a kids RPM of only about $0.3. On top of that, kids/COPPA rules limit ad types (not verified in this session). Weak for ad revenue.
- **Higher value per view, more defensible:** long-form AI-assisted documentary, history and explainer channels with real scripting and narration. Finance and education explainers pay the most but are the most competitive.
- **Saturated or high-risk:**
  - Fake trailers have been terminated.
  - Reddit-story TTS, Bigfoot-style AI vlogs and generic "satisfying" compilations: when blueprints are sold on Gumroad, the opportunity is usually past its peak.
  - Generic AI slop: removals in late 2025 and 2026 show that YouTube will delete whole channels, not just demonetize them.
- **For a BiH-based creator:** making content in English (or Spanish or German) for Tier-1 audiences matters more than where the creator lives. A Bosnian-language (BCS) audience would sit in the "emerging/low-RPM" band. That is an inference from the geography RPM data, since no BiH-specific RPM data was found.

### Gaps
- I found no verified, independent data on how these specific formats perform or what they earn: AI ASMR, AI music channels, Reddit stories, "what if" videos, meme pages, AI cinematic shorts. Search returned only vendor and SEO blogs, and those were not usable.
- Kapwing's channel-level revenue is modelled from Social Blade, not actual payouts.
- No 2026 Reddit (r/NewTubers, r/PartneredYoutube, r/aivideo) first-hand data could be retrieved, because WebFetch was down and the search budget was exhausted.
- An OutlierKit timeline claims 11 channels were terminated in January 2026 and that "4.7B+ views wiped, ~$10M revenue eliminated across 16 channels". These claims were not confirmed in major outlets. — [OutlierKit (unverified)](https://alpha.outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-2026/)

## 2. Platform monetization programs and eligibility for creators in Bosnia and Herzegovina, plus other ways to earn and get paid

### Takeaway
**YouTube is the one big platform ad-revenue program clearly open to BiH residents.** Bosnia and Herzegovina appears on the YouTube Partner Program availability list.

**TikTok Creator Rewards is not available in BiH.** It covers only about 7–8 large markets.

**Facebook Content Monetization is invite-only.** No source confirms BiH. Treat it as unavailable unless an invite shows up in the Professional Dashboard.

**YouTube's July 2025 "inauthentic content" rename, the January 2026 anti-"AI slop" push and the May 2026 automatic AI labels mean AI channels must show real human value added.** Money from outside the platforms (sponsors, affiliate, digital products, Whop/Patreon) has to go through Payoneer, PayPal or Whop-style payout routes. Those mostly support BiH, but each one needs checking.

### Cited Findings
**YouTube Partner Program (YPP): available in BiH**
- The YPP availability list includes Bosnia and Herzegovina, between Bolivia and Brazil. Caveat: confirmed through a cached or translated copy of the help page; the live page could not be opened. — [YouTube Help: YPP availability (Filipino version)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7101720?hl=fil)
- YouTube has a channel-membership price list specific to Bosnia and Herzegovina, priced in convertible marks (BAM). — [YouTube Help: membership pricing, BA](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10120241?hl=en-GB&co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DBA)
- BiH is on YouTube's "monetized markets" list. This list is about where advertisers can target ads, not creator eligibility. — [YouTube Help: monetized markets](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1342206?hl=en)
- Bosnian coverage (older) reported that YPP opens at 500 subscribers for fan-funding features, while ad revenue keeps the existing thresholds. — [Radiosarajevo.ba](https://radiosarajevo.ba/magazin/tech/od-sada-lakse-do-zarade-na-youtube/500636)

**YouTube "inauthentic content" update (effective 15 July 2025)**
- The YPP "repetitious content" policy was renamed "inauthentic content" to better identify mass-produced and repetitive material. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/youtube-updates-monetisation-policies-ai-and-repetitive-content-ban-begins-july-15-1.500192660); [Plagiarism Today, 8 Jul 2025](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/07/08/youtube-targets-inauthentic-content/)

Rene Ritchie said reaction and clip channels were not expected to be affected. — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/youtube-clarifies-monetisation-policy-targeting-inauthentic-content-9493155); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-clarifies-inauthentic-content-policy-changes/)
- **Changes that are not enough:** borrowed content with minimal changes, such as adding background music, changing playback speed or cropping. **Formats in scope:** templated or highly repetitive formats, especially in Shorts. — [SubSub](https://www.subsub.io/blog/youtube-inauthentic-content-policy-2025); [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/7/youtube-updates-its-policy-to-demonetize-inauthentic-mass-produced-ai-generated-content)
- **Neal Mohan's annual letter (January 2026)** named "AI slop" a priority. YouTube is "actively building on our established systems that have been very successful in combating spam and clickbait, and reducing the spread of low-quality, repetitive content". — [Digital Music News, 22 Jan 2026](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2026/01/22/youtube-ceo-ai-slop-2026-comments/)

  - For photorealistic or meaningfully AI-altered videos, the label now sits directly below the player (long-form) or as an overlay on the video (Shorts).
  - YouTube auto-applies labels when creators fail to disclose, using internal signals plus C2PA metadata and SynthID.
  - Creators can dispute labels in Studio, but labels on content made with Veo or Dream Screen may be permanent.
  - YouTube says labels do not directly affect monetization or recommendations.
  - — [TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/platform-updates/youtube-automatic-ai-labels-disclosure-may-2026); [Minimatters](https://minimatters.com/youtube-ai-content-labeling-update-in-may-2026/); [Creator Handbook](https://www.creatorhandbook.net/youtube-expands-automatic-ai-labeling-system/)
- Realistic altered or synthetic content must be disclosed. Examples include replacing a face or synthetically generating a person's voice to narrate. YouTube said it would consider enforcement against creators who consistently fail to disclose. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/companies/youtube-mandates-labelling-of-synthetic-ai-generated-content); [Jasper (summary)](https://www.jasper.ai/blog/youtube-synthetic-content-disclosures)

### Inferences
- **Monetization order of attack for a BiH resident:**
  1. YouTube (long-form plus Shorts) for ad revenue and memberships.
  2. Off-platform income for everything else: sponsorships, affiliate links, Whop/Gumroad digital products, Patreon.
  3. TikTok and Instagram/Facebook mainly as free traffic sources. Their creator funds are probably closed to BiH, but brand deals, LIVE gifts and subscriptions may work.
- Workarounds such as "foreign-registered" TikTok or Facebook accounts (sold on Fiverr) and VPNs risk bans and break platform terms. Do not recommend them.
- The July 2025 policy plus the 2026 enforcement mean AI channels can still be monetized, but only with clear human value added: original scripts, real narration or commentary, editing and a consistent series identity. Low-effort templated Shorts carry the risk of losing the whole channel.

## 3. AI influencers and virtual creators: verified case studies, how they are made, how they monetize, and disclosure/legal limits

### Takeaway
"Verified" revenue figures for AI influencers barely exist. Every headline number either comes from the agency or creator selling the concept (Aitana, around €3–10K a month in 2023) or is a third-party estimate (Lil Miquela at $8–12M a year, from a vendor blog). The most credible money signal is the AI-adult subscription market. On Fanvue, AI creators are about 15% of revenue, and the platform's annual run-rate reportedly reached $100–200M by 2025–2026.

Consistent characters are now built with trained-identity tools (Higgsfield Soul ID, Flux LoRA) plus reference or edit tools (Nano Banana Pro, Flux Kontext, Midjourney Omni Reference), then animated in Kling or similar. **From 2 August 2026, EU AI Act Article 50 requires deepfakes and AI-manipulated content to be disclosed.**

### Cited Findings
**Aitana López (The Clueless, Barcelona). Figures are from 2023.**
- Created by The Clueless agency (Rubén Cruz) after the founders had trouble working with human models.
- Claimed earnings: more than €1,000 per advert and €3,000–10,000 a month. Another report says about €4,000 a month from selling lingerie photos, including on Fanvue.
- She had about 122–124K Instagram followers.
- — [Business Insider NL](https://www.businessinsider.nl/an-agency-created-an-ai-model-who-earns-up-to-11000-a-month-because-it-was-tired-of-influencers-who-have-egos); [Fortune (mirror), 23 Nov 2023](https://dc.fortune.com/europe/2023/11/23/spanish-influencer-agency-earned-11000-ai-model-posers/); [Inquirer Tech](https://technology.inquirer.net/?p=130142); [Vozpópuli](https://www.vozpopuli.com/espana/influencer-inteligencia-artificial-4000-euros.html); [Noticias RCN](https://www.noticiasrcn.com/tendencias/la-influencer-creada-con-inteligencia-artificial-457318)
- An analysis notes these are agency claims, not audited accounts, and that a typical month was nearer €3,000 than €10,000. The source is a company blog. — [DreamPixelForge](https://www.dreampixelforge.com/blog/how-to-make-money-with-ai-influencers)

## 4. Making content without being on camera (AI avatars, voice clones, faceless styles) and what "Genjutsu" is

### Takeaway
**"Genjutsu" is real.** Higgsfield Genjutsu is a video-to-video AI model, reportedly launched on 1 September 2026. You film any reference clip (yourself, a dancer, a scene), and it rebuilds the character, location, outfit or product from reference images while keeping the original motion, timing and camera work. That lets a creator perform on camera and come out as an AI character. It is almost certainly the tool the user meant. It is not a text-to-video generator and is described as not a face-swap or identity-swap tool.

### Cited Findings
- **What it is:** Higgsfield Genjutsu is a video-to-video tool with two modes.
  - Motion Transfer keeps motion, camera and timing, and rebuilds the scene, character or style from references.
  - Object Swap changes one selected element (character, outfit, product, location) and preserves the rest of the shot.
  - — [Higgsfield blog](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/higgsfield-genjutsu); [Higgsfield Genjutsu page](https://higgsfield.ai/genjutsu); [AIChief](https://aichief.com/ai-video-tools/higgsfield-genjutsu/); [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/en/higgsfield-genjutsu)
- **Launch:** reportedly 1 September 2026, listed on Product Hunt on 3 September 2026. — [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/en/higgsfield-genjutsu); [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/1005829); [Digg](https://digg.com/tech/d54f4b58-9d43-4dbd-8c23-d0b29c1b529f)
- **Specs:**
  - Reference videos of 3–30 seconds and up to 40 reference images.
  - Output up to 1080p.
  - Presets can work with no prompt.
  - It is not a from-scratch text-to-video generator and not a face-swap or identity-swap tool.
  - Quality drops on fast movement (hands and heads lose sharpness).
  - — [AIChief](https://aichief.com/ai-video-tools/higgsfield-genjutsu/); [T-J.ru](https://t-j.ru/higgsfield-genjutsu/); [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/en/higgsfield-genjutsu)
- **Pricing (conflicting):** one listing says a paid subscription is required with no free tier. A comparison site says prepaid credit packs start at $4.99, with credits scaling by clip length and resolution. — [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/en/higgsfield-genjutsu); [AI Indigo](https://aiindigo.com/compare/genjutsu-ai-vs-kling-ai)
- **Avatar use:** a 12 September 2026 tutorial covers turning your own videos into an animated AI avatar with this kind of workflow. — [HowDoIUseAI, 12 Sep 2026](https://www.howdoiuseai.com/blog/2026-09-12-how-to-turn-your-videos-into-an-animated-ai-avatar)
- **Character pipelines:** Higgsfield also offers Soul ID (trained identity), Kling 3.0 animation and Lipsync Studio, which together give a full "AI persona that talks" pipeline (see Section 3). — [Higgsfield Academy](https://higgsfield.ai/academy/how-to-use/how-to-create-ai-influencer)

### Inferences
- **Workflow for a creator who doesn't want to show their face:**
  1. Record yourself (phone, any background) acting, talking or dancing.
  2. Use Genjutsu Motion Transfer with reference images of a consistent AI character (built with Soul ID or Nano Banana).
  3. Add a cloned or synthetic voice, or keep your real voice.
  - The result keeps the human performance, timing and acting, which also helps meet YouTube's "original value" test. Fully synthetic slop does not.
- **Other ways to stay off camera (no data verified this session):**
  - AI "digital twin" avatars (HeyGen, Synthesia, Captions) for talking-head explainers.
  - Voice-over over stock footage, B-roll or AI B-roll.
  - Screen recordings.
  - Animated or illustrated characters.

## 5. Growth tactics: how faceless pages get millions of views

### Takeaway
The evidence is thin, but it points to one pattern. **Faceless and AI pages grow on Shorts and TikTok feeds,** which push a lot of AI and brainrot to new users. They grow through:
- high-volume, recurring series formats (a recurring character or channel "universe");
- curiosity or controversy hooks;
- relentless re-editing toward what the algorithm rewards.

The same tactics (templated, mass-produced, misleading titles) are what YouTube's inauthentic-content and spam/metadata policies now punish.

### Cited Findings
- About 21–33% of the first 500 Shorts on a new YouTube account were AI slop or brainrot. Feeds actively test this content on new users. — [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/one-in-five-youtube-shorts-shown-to-new-users-is-ai-generated-slop-study-finds/); [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/ai-generated-content-accounts-for-third-of-youtube-shorts-feed-kapwing-research-shows/)
- The top AI channels run single recurring concepts or characters at huge volume: a monkey "best friend" character (Bandar Apna Dost), a French bulldog (Pouty Frenchie), and "Three Minutes Wisdom". — [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/india-youtube-bandar-apna-dost-channel-global-ai-video-charts-slop-content-125123100396_1.html)
- Screen Culture used a team of editors who kept re-editing videos to exploit the algorithm, along with clickbait "fake trailer" titles. That ended in termination for misleading metadata. — [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/?p=7915970); [OECD.AI](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2025-12-18-1864)
- Bigfoot-vlog sellers market "the exact 12 videos that built the channel". That is a serialized character format posted to TikTok and YouTube at the same time. — [BigfootBoyz Gumroad](https://bigfootboyz.gumroad.com/l/aivlogs)
- YouTube's July 2025 update specifically puts templated or highly repetitive Shorts formats in scope for demonetization. — [SubSub](https://www.subsub.io/blog/youtube-inauthentic-content-policy-2025)
- TikTok's Creator Rewards favors original videos longer than 1 minute and "qualified views". — [ShortSync](https://www.shortsync.app/resources/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-2026); [Bosnamedia](https://www.bosnamedia.info/2025/04/koliko-se-stvarno-moze-zaraditi-na-tiktoku/)

### Inferences
- **A playbook that holds up:**
  1. Build one recurring character or series ("universe") rather than random one-off videos.
  2. Post daily Shorts, Reels and TikToks as top-of-funnel traffic.
  3. Repurpose the best ideas into long-form YouTube for RPM.
  4. Use strong curiosity hooks in the first second.
  5. Cross-post every clip to all three platforms.
  6. Move viewers to owned assets (email list, Whop, product) because platform risk is high.
- **What to avoid:** misleading titles, fake trailers, faked news or impersonation, and identical templated uploads. These are the patterns tied to demonetization and terminations.

### Gaps
- I found no rigorous, citable 2025–2026 data on what drives views for faceless pages: optimal posting frequency, hook retention benchmarks, or how cross-posting changes reach. First-hand data from Reddit and Tubefilter could not be retrieved because WebFetch was down and the search budget was exhausted.
- I found no data on buying or selling pages (page-flipping marketplaces) or on Facebook meme-page economics.

## 6. Realistic numbers: time to monetization, income distribution, verified examples

### Takeaway
**Earnings are extremely concentrated.** Only about 2% (278) of roughly 15,000 top-trending channels worldwide were AI-only, and even the very best earn single-digit millions of dollars a year (estimated). Most AI and faceless channels earn little: Shorts pay cents to about $1.50 per 1,000 views, and audiences outside Tier-1 markets pay 60–80% less. AI-influencer income claims are €3K–$60K a month at the very top, all unaudited.
